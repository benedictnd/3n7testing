Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required during registration/sign-up? +
Please upload the required documents to an online drive (e.g., Google Drive, OneDrive) and ensure that your license has not expired. The screening process for your license typically takes around 12-24 hours.
What if I train with more than 2 teams? +

Head coach, assistant coach, and athletes: You can register a maximum of 3 teams (e.g., professional club, regional team, and national team).
Example: Long Marine Beach Surf Club, Bali Regional Team, Indonesia National Team.

Assistant coaches and part-timers: You can register a maximum of 5 teams (professional club and regional team, if applicable).

How does my team get the training program from me? +
During registration, your athletes will be required to enter the club's email or your email address. This also applies to stakeholders and parents. By providing your email, athletes will automatically receive an email notification linking them to the training program.
I am not yet part of a professional team, just for exercise & staying healthy. Can I still register? +
Yes, everyone is welcome to participate! Whether you're a sports enthusiast or simply looking to improve your health, you can register and join. Everyone who registers is considered a hero of 3&7.
Are parents and stakeholders able to register for monitoring? +
Yes, absolutely! Parents and stakeholders play a crucial role. During sign-up, simply select the "stakeholders" option and connect with your athlete by entering their registered email address. This allows you to monitor their progress and stay involved.

Parents will receive a "find club/community" feature for their children.
