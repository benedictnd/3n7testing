# 3&7 Training Platform - Project Brief

## Project Overview
The 3&7 Training Platform is a web application designed to facilitate and track athletic training regimens, with a particular focus on enabling users to schedule, record, and verify both integrated and independent training sessions. The platform aims to bridge the gap between coaches and athletes by providing tools for seamless communication, progress tracking, and verification of training activities.

## Core Requirements

### Functional Requirements
1. **User Authentication**: Secure login for athletes, coaches, and administrators
2. **Training Session Management**:
   - Integrated training session scheduling and attendance tracking
   - Independent training session logging with proof submission
   - Calendar view of all training activities
3. **Proof Verification System**:
   - Photo/video upload capabilities
   - Geolocation verification
   - Timestamp validation
4. **Communication Tools**:
   - Automated notifications for scheduled sessions
   - Coach feedback mechanism
   - Email notifications for proof verification
5. **Analytics Dashboard**:
   - Training completion statistics
   - Progress visualization
   - Performance metrics tracking

### Non-Functional Requirements
1. **UI/UX Quality**: 
   - 95%+ score on mobile optimization
   - 95%+ score on component consistency
   - Dark mode support
   - Responsive design for all device sizes
2. **Performance**:
   - Page load times under 2 seconds
   - Optimized image loading
   - Skeleton loading states for better perceived performance
3. **Accessibility**:
   - WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
   - Keyboard navigation support
   - Screen reader compatibility
4. **Security**:
   - Data encryption
   - Secure authentication practices
   - Regular security audits

## Target Users
1. **Athletes**: Individuals who need to track and verify their training activities
2. **Coaches**: Training professionals who oversee athlete progress and provide feedback
3. **Administrators**: System managers who oversee platform operations

## Key Features
1. **Dual Training Types**: Support for both integrated (coach-led) and independent (self-directed) training sessions
2. **Proof Submission System**: Multiple verification methods to validate independent training
3. **Interactive Calendar**: Visual representation of all scheduled and completed training
4. **Notification System**: Automated alerts for upcoming sessions and verification results
5. **Performance Dashboard**: Visual analytics of training progress and achievements

## Success Metrics
1. **User Adoption**: 90%+ athlete participation rate
2. **Training Completion**: 85%+ verified independent training sessions
3. **User Satisfaction**: 4.5/5 star average rating from athletes and coaches
4. **Technical Performance**: Meet all non-functional requirements with 95%+ compliance
