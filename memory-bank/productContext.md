# 3&7 Training Platform - Product Context

## Problem Statement
Traditional training methods lack structured data collection, making it difficult to track progress and optimize performance efficiently. Athletes and coaches need comprehensive tools that bridge the gap between physical training and data-driven insights.

## Solution Approach
The 3&7 Platform provides a unified ecosystem where:
- Athletes can follow structured training plans and submit proof of independent sessions
- Coaches can monitor team and individual progress with real-time updates
- Stakeholders can observe development and access comprehensive reports
- All users benefit from a modern, accessible interface that works across all devices

## User Experience Goals
- Create an intuitive, responsive interface that requires minimal training
- Provide immediate feedback for all user actions
- Ensure accessibility for users of all abilities
- Optimize performance for varying network conditions
- Support both light and dark themes for different usage environments

## Value Proposition
"Train, Recover, Acclaimed" - The 3&7 platform transforms athletic training through technology, enhancing the quality of sports development through innovation and data-driven approaches.
